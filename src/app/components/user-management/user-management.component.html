Enterprise Cryptographic Architecture Note:

Argon2id is a one-way cryptographic hash function, NOT reversible encryption. Hashes are mathematically designed with pre-image resistance so they cannot be decrypted back into plaintext. In enterprise systems (OWASP & NIST SP 800-63B standards), storing irreversible hashes ensures that even a complete database breach will never expose user passwords.
